⚑ Government Affiliation Disclaimer The Loyola School Harnaut mobile application is a private Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) platform for school management. This application does NOT represent any government entity, state education department, or official board. It is a private tool used exclusively for administrative and communication purposes by Loyola School Harnaut.

1 Introduction

Loyola School Harnaut ("We," "Us," or "Our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and manage data through the Loyola School Harnaut mobile application, developed and managed for the exclusive use of our students, parents, and staff.

2 Account Provisioning & Registration

The Loyola School Harnaut app is a closed platform. There is no public account registration or self-service sign-up.

  • Accounts are created and managed exclusively by authorized School Administrators.
  • Login credentials are provided directly to students, parents, and staff by the school administration.

3 Data Deletion & Account Closure

📋 How to Request Data Deletion Users have the right to request deletion of their personal information. Since accounts are managed by the school, you may:
  1. Contact the school administrative office at Loyola School Harnaut to deactivate your record.
  2. Submit a direct deletion request to our support team at loyolaschoolharnaut@gmail.com.

Upon a valid request, personal identifiers (Name, Phone, Email, Photos) will be removed from our active databases within 30 days, unless retention is legally required for academic or financial auditing purposes.

4 Information We Collect

We process the following data to provide school-related services:

  • Personal Information: Name, Student/Staff IDs, Phone Number, and Email Address.
  • Precise Location: Used strictly for Live Bus Tracking services for authorized parents and students.
  • Media Access: Camera and device storage are accessed only when a user chooses to upload profile photos, homework assignments, or school documents.
  • Device Information: Device model, OS version, and unique identifiers for security and performance monitoring.

5 How We Use Your Data

  • To manage student records, attendance, and academic progress.
  • To facilitate communication between school staff, parents, and students.
  • To enable live bus tracking for student safety.
  • To send school notices, circulars, and event updates.
  • To ensure security and proper functioning of the platform.

6 Data Security

  • All data is encrypted in transit using SSL/TLS (HTTPS).
  • Access to sensitive school data is restricted to authorized personnel only.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share user personal data with third-party advertisers.

7 Children's Privacy

As this application is used in an educational setting, we store data relating to minors (students). Loyola School Harnaut is responsible for obtaining appropriate parental or guardian consent before uploading student data to the platform, in compliance with applicable child protection and data protection laws of India.

8 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any significant changes will be communicated through the application or via official school notices. Continued use of the app after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
